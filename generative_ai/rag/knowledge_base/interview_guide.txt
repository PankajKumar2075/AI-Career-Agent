TECHNICAL INTERVIEW PREPARATION GUIDE

Introduction:
Technical interviews evaluate programming knowledge,
problem-solving ability, technical fundamentals and communication.

Data Structures and Algorithms:
Practice arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks, queues,
trees, graphs, hashing, recursion and dynamic programming.

Programming:
Understand object-oriented programming, functions,
memory management, exception handling and complexity analysis.

Computer Science Fundamentals:
Prepare operating systems, database management systems,
computer networks and software engineering.

Project Preparation:
Understand project architecture, technology choices,
important features, challenges and your individual contribution.

Practice:
Solve coding problems regularly, explain your approach
clearly and participate in mock interviews.

Resume Preparation:
Keep your resume concise, highlight measurable achievements,
include relevant projects and mention technical skills accurately.
